**Project: Drift Game in the City**  

**Description**  
In this Unity game, you drive through a city and can drift. The city is infinite and generates itself as you drive further.

**Solutions**  

- **Infinite City**  
  - **Solution**: The city is procedurally generated.  
  - **Why**: This keeps the world infinite without consuming too much memory.  

- **Drift Mechanic**  
  - **Solution**: Custom vehicle physics for smooth drifting.  
  - **Why**: To make drifting fun and challenging.  

- **Performance Optimization**  
  - **Solution**: Object pooling for buildings.  
  - **Why**: For better performance and smoother gameplay.  

**Reflection**  

- **More Variety in the City**  
  - **Improvement**: Add more diverse buildings.  
  - **Why**: To avoid repetition.  

- **Better Drift Feedback**  
  - **Improvement**: Add more visual and audio effects while drifting.  
  - **Why**: To make drifting more engaging.  

- **Progression System**  
  - **Improvement**: Add scoring and challenges.  
  - **Why**: To increase motivation and replayability.  

**Requirements**  
- Unity version 6 LTS or higher  
- A system with at least 4GB RAM
